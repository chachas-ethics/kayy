01 · The Idea

A safari designed around you.

The Serengeti does not need a rigid itinerary. Your days can begin slowly with coffee overlooking the plains, move into the wilderness when the light is right, and end beneath an open African sky.

Whether your priority is wildlife, photography, landscape, conservation, solitude or simply time away from everything, the journey is shaped accordingly. Kavora handles the details behind the experience so that the journey itself feels effortless.

The wilderness is not a backdrop. It is the journey.

06 · When to Go

There is no single perfect season.

The right time depends on what you want from the journey. Weather, wildlife patterns, migration movements and your personal preferences all influence how we shape the experience.

Dry Season

Clearer conditions and strong wildlife viewing opportunities in many areas.

Green Season

Lush landscapes, dramatic skies and a quieter atmosphere in many parts of the ecosystem.

Great Migration

A journey can be timed around migration patterns, while recognising that wildlife movements remain natural.

Calving Season

A distinctive period in the ecosystem for travellers interested in predator-prey dynamics and new life.
