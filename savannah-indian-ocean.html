From Savannah to the Indian Ocean

A private journey from the wilderness of Tanzania to the shores of Zanzibar.

Begin among the great landscapes of northern Tanzania before exchanging the rhythm of the savannah for warm Indian Ocean waters. One journey. Two completely different worlds.

01 — The Concept

From wilderness to water.

Start with Tanzania's wildlife-rich landscapes.

Then slow everything down.

After days of safari, the journey moves towards Zanzibar — white sand, warm water, historic streets and long evenings by the ocean.

The contrast is what makes the journey.

02 — The Safari

Begin in the wild.

The safari portion of the journey can move through different landscapes depending on what you want to experience. These are possibilities rather than prescribed stops.

Possibility 01

Serengeti

Wildlife and expansive landscapes. A place where the scale of the wilderness becomes part of the experience.

Possibility 02

Ngorongoro

The dramatic crater and one of Tanzania's most distinctive ecosystems.

Possibility 03

Tarangire

Ancient baobabs, elephants and a quieter safari atmosphere.

03 — The Transition

From dust to salt air.

One moment you are watching elephants move across the plains.

The next, you are walking barefoot beside the Indian Ocean. The transition itself becomes part of the journey.

04 — Zanzibar

Then, exhale.

Zanzibar becomes the second chapter of the journey. Not simply an add-on after safari, but an opportunity to change pace completely.

The Coast

Barefoot days.

Private beaches, swimming, sailing and slow afternoons beside warm Indian Ocean waters.

Stone Town

History in every street.

Architecture, markets, history and culture woven through the old streets of Zanzibar.

The Indian Ocean

Into the blue.

Snorkelling, diving, dhow sailing and marine experiences designed around your pace.

Island Dining

Evenings by the ocean.

Private dinners, beachfront meals and locally influenced cuisine become part of the experience.
