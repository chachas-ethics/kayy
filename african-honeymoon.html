Timing matters.

There is no single perfect time for an African honeymoon. There is the right time for you.

Safari

Seasonal conditions influence landscapes, wildlife movement and the rhythm of safari.

Wildlife

Different periods offer different possibilities, from migration activity to quieter wilderness.

Weather

Weather patterns help shape the ideal balance between safari and coastal time.

Beach

Migration

For couples drawn to the Great Migration, timing becomes an important part of the planning.

Privacy & Value

Seasonality can also influence availability, privacy and overall journey cost.
