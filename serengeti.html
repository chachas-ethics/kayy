The Serengeti

A landscape that asks you to slow down.

Kavora approaches the Serengeti as a private experience — selecting camps, routes and experiences according to your interests, pace and preferred level of seclusion.

When to go

The Serengeti changes.

There is no single moment that defines the Serengeti. Landscapes, wildlife and conditions shift throughout the year, which is why Kavora considers timing as part of the journey itself.

Jan — Mar

Calving Season

Green landscapes, young wildlife and heightened predator activity create an especially compelling time to experience the southern Serengeti.

Apr — May

The Green Season

Lush landscapes, dramatic skies and generally quieter conditions create a different expression of the Serengeti.

Jun — Oct

The Dry Season

Clearer conditions, concentrated wildlife and strong game-viewing opportunities make this one of the most popular periods to travel.

Nov — Dec

The Short Rains

The landscape begins to renew itself as seasonal rains return, bringing fresh vegetation and changing wildlife patterns.

Migration movements are natural and constantly shifting. Your journey should follow the wilderness, rather than promise it.

Before You Go

The details, considered.

The Serengeti rewards preparation, but the details should never overshadow the experience. Kavora handles the practical considerations with you during the planning process.

Your journey can begin through Tanzania's major international gateways before connecting onward by road or scheduled and chartered safari flights. Final routing depends on your itinerary, accommodation and preferred pace.
A minimum of three to four nights allows enough time to settle into the landscape. Five to seven nights provides greater flexibility and a more unhurried experience. Longer stays can be designed around migration timing, remote camps and additional destinations.
Lightweight neutral clothing, comfortable footwear, sun protection, layers for cooler mornings and evenings, binoculars and camera equipment are useful starting points. Luggage requirements can vary when travelling by safari aircraft.
Connectivity varies considerably between camps and remote areas. Some properties provide reliable Wi-Fi or mobile coverage, while others intentionally offer a more disconnected experience.
Family suitability depends on the property, age of the children and style of safari. Private guiding and carefully selected camps can provide greater flexibility for families. These considerations should be discussed during planning.
The Serengeti offers exceptional opportunities for wildlife and landscape photography. Private vehicles, longer stays and experienced guides can provide greater flexibility for guests with specific photographic interests.
Accessibility varies by camp, vehicle, terrain and transfer arrangements. Individual requirements should be discussed with Kavora before confirming a journey so that suitable properties and logistics can be considered.
