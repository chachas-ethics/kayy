The Island

An island with more than one rhythm.

Zanzibar is not simply somewhere to finish a safari. It is a destination in its own right.

The island moves between Indian Ocean horizons, centuries-old architecture, Swahili culture, spice gardens and quiet stretches of coast. Its history is visible in the streets of Stone Town, while its natural character reveals itself offshore and along the beaches.

Kavora approaches Zanzibar as a slower chapter of the journey. Carefully selected properties, private experiences and unhurried days allow the island to become whatever you need it to be: restorative, cultural, romantic or simply quiet.

When to Go

There is no single Zanzibar season.

Timing depends on what you want from the island. Your Travel Designer will consider weather patterns, ocean conditions and the experiences you want to include.

Jun — Oct

Dry Season

Often favoured for beach escapes, outdoor exploration and generally drier conditions.

Nov — Dec

Short Rains

A transitional period with changing conditions and renewed landscapes.

Jan — Mar

Warm Ocean Months

Warm conditions and strong opportunities for ocean-focused experiences, depending on local conditions.

Apr — May

Long Rains

A quieter period with more variable rainfall and a different rhythm across the island.

Conditions vary across the island. Ocean activities, diving and marine experiences should always be considered alongside current local conditions.
